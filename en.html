Privacy Policy

La Taquería del Fin del Mundo · ZamLab Studios · Last updated: November 18, 2026 · Versión en español

In short: the game does not ask for an account, does not collect personal data, and has no ads or analytics. It only stores your progress, in-game savings, upgrades, outfit and purchases on your own device. If you buy something (optional), Huawei processes the payment.

1. Who we are

ZamLab Studios ("we") develops La Taquería del Fin del Mundo ("the game"), in which a taco cook serves monster customers at night. For any questions about this policy, write to zamlab.studios@gmail.com.

2. What is stored and where

The game stores the following in your device's local storage:

  • your progress in each campaign (the night you reached, the stars and the money record for each night) and your endless-mode record;
  • your savings (the in-game currency earned at the end of each night) and the kitchen upgrades bought with them;
  • the uniform, tablecloth and plate you chose in the wardrobe;
  • whether sound is on or off;
  • what you have purchased (Leyendas de Medianoche, Pack de apoyo al taquero or Posada de Monstruos), so it stays unlocked even offline.

Savings and upgrades can only be earned by playing; they cannot be bought with real money.

The game does not ask for any name. This information is not sent to us or to any third party. It stays on your device.

3. What we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your real name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, advertising identifiers or usage data. The game has no user accounts, shows no ads and includes no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

4. Permissions

The Android app only requests the internet and network state permissions, required by Huawei's in-app purchase service, and vibration, so the phone can vibrate at some moments of the game. The whole game is bundled in the app and works offline; it does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

5. Purchases

The "La Taquería" campaign (7 nights), endless mode, kitchen upgrades and the basic wardrobe are free. In the AppGallery version you can optionally buy, each with a one-time payment:

  • Leyendas de Medianoche: a 6-night campaign with new customers and meats;
  • Pack de apoyo al taquero: extra uniforms, tablecloths and plates (cosmetic only);
  • Posada de Monstruos: a 5-night Christmas campaign with new customers, meats and outfits.

Purchases and payments are processed by Huawei (HUAWEI IAP) under its own terms and privacy policy: we do not receive your payment or contact details, only the signed confirmation that the purchase was completed, which we verify on your device before unlocking the item.

Huawei keeps the record of your purchase in your HUAWEI ID account. If you reinstall the game or change phones, you can recover it with "Restaurar compras" (Restore purchases) without paying again. For billing or refund questions, contact Huawei AppGallery support.

6. Third parties and links

The game does not share information with third parties, except what is needed for purchases: Huawei (HMS Core / AppGallery services) processes payment and HUAWEI ID account data under its own policy, independent of us. If you download the game from an app store (for example, AppGallery), the store may process data about your download under its own policies.

The "Más juegos" (More games) button opens our other games' pages in AppGallery, and the "Privacidad" (Privacy) button opens this page in your browser. Those pages have their own policies; the game does not send them any of your data. If you play a web version, the hosting site may log technical visits (such as the IP address), like any website; the game adds no analytics.

7. Children

We do not collect personal data from anyone, including children. The game features cartoon-style monsters (a vampire, a zombie, skeletons, Mexican legends), with no blood or violence; check the age rating shown in the store before installing it.

8. Your control: deleting your data

You can delete the game's saved data at any time from Android settings (Settings → Apps → La Taquería del Fin del Mundo → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the app. Since we have no copy of your information, there is nothing you need to ask us to delete. Clearing data does not cancel your purchases: Huawei keeps them and you can restore them.

9. Security

Your game data stays on your device and is protected by the device's own security measures (screen lock, encryption, etc.).

10. Changes to this policy

If we change it, we will publish the new version here with its date. If a change affects how your information is handled (for example, if the game ever included ads), we will say so on this page before it takes effect.
